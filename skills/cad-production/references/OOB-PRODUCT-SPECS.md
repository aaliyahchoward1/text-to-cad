### Cap Specifications

**M24 Dropper Assembly (standard for 30–100ml bottles)**
- Thread depth: ≥36mm (1.5× diameter minimum for engagement)
- Bulb material: Silicone (natural, latex-free)
- Tip: Stainless steel needle, 0.5mm gauge
- Compression force: 5–8N
- Delivery volume: 0.5ml/squeeze ±10%
- Cap cost @ 10K: $0.35–$0.40

**M27 Thread (for 100ml+ bottles)**
- Larger bulb for higher delivery volume
- Thread depth: ≥40mm
- Typical cost @ 10K: $0.45–$0.50

### Label Specifications

**Circumference-Based Wraps**
- Finish: Matte, embossed (no gloss for premium feel)
- Adhesive: Eco-friendly, removable
- Material: 150gsm kraft or recycled paper
- Cost per label @ 10K: $0.12–$0.15

| Size | Diameter | Depth | Shell Material | Cost @ 5K |
|------|----------|-------|----------------|-----------|

| Standard | 45mm | 25mm | Polystyrene | $0.55 |

**Hinge Specifications:**
- Pin diameter: 2.0–2.5mm (stainless steel)
- Engagement depth: 8–12mm
- Snap fit force: 5–10N (user-satisfying "click")
- Cycles to failure: >10,000 (cosmetic use standard)

**Latch Options:**
- Spring latch (polypropylene, $0.08/unit @ 5K)
- Magnetic latch (rare earth, $0.15/unit @ 5K)
- Snap fit (no hardware, $0.02/unit)

### Fabric Lining

- Material: Velvet, silk, or cotton
- Application: Adhesive-backed (contact cement or hot-melt)
- Color options: Black, burgundy, rose gold, ivory
- Cost @ 5K: $0.12–$0.20/unit
- Assembly labor (hand-lining): $0.20–$0.30/unit

**Assembly Process:**
1. Mold clamshell parts (injection)
2. Insert hinge pins into female sockets
3. Apply fabric lining to interior cavity
4. Attach latch hardware (if not snap-fit)
5. Final snap-test and quality inspection
6. Total assembly time: 3–5 minutes/unit

## Cost Model Defaults

### Material Costs (Polypropylene, Polystyrene)
- Per gram: $0.012–$0.018
- Minimum resin cost: 2–4% of total per-unit

### Injection Molding
- Tooling cost: $5K–$15K (single-cavity, standard products)
- Cycle time: 40–90 seconds
- Labor: $0.08–$0.15/unit
- Overhead/packaging: $0.10–$0.20/unit

### CNC Machining (Aluminum)
- Setup: $500–$1500
- Per-unit: $8–$15 (material + labor combined)
- Finishing (anodize): $0.50–$1.50/unit additional

### Assembly (Multi-Part Products)
- Hand assembly: $0.20–$0.50/unit
- Automated assembly: $0.05–$0.15/unit
- QA/testing: $0.10–$0.20/unit

## Manufacturing Lead Times

| Process | Tooling | First Shot | Full Volume (10K) |
|---------|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| Injection Molding | 3–4 weeks | 1 week | 2–3 weeks after tooling approval |
| CNC Machining | 1–2 weeks (setup) | 2–3 days | 4–6 weeks @ full capacity |
| Metal Finishing (Anodize) | None | 3–5 days | 2–3 weeks depending on queue |
| Assembly | None | 1–2 days | 2–4 weeks depending on labor |

**Overall Timeline to Production:**
- Simple molded parts (bottles, caps): 6–8 weeks from approved CAD
- Complex hardware (AstroPodra): 8–12 weeks (machining + finishing)
- Multi-assembly products (GlamGrab): 7–10 weeks (mold + assembly setup)
